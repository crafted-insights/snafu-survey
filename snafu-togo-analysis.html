Key Talking Points

THE DROP
To-go revenue fell from $13,127 → $9,649 in June YoY — a $3,478 loss. Cans account for $1,958 of that gap; crowlers $1,308. Both are shrinking as a % of every dollar that comes in.
DRAFT ISN'T THE CAUSE
Draft revenue in June was $14,866 this year vs $14,795 last year — essentially flat. Guests aren't choosing draft over cans/crowlers. Staff just aren't suggesting to-go at the point of sale.
THE OPPORTUNITY
Crowlers hit 15.8% in July 2025 — nearly double today's 8.7%. Getting back to Jun 2025 to-go mix (~41.7%) at current traffic would recover ~$2,000/month with zero new customers needed.
